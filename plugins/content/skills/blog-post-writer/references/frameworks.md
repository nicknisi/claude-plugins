# Narrative Frameworks — Diagnostic Only

The 27 framework files in this directory are **no longer a menu to pick from before
writing.** They are vocabulary for critiquing a draft that already exists.

## Why the menu was removed

This skill used to open with "choose a narrative framework," offering 27 options
with a quick-match table. That step produced the post that got called AI slop, and
it did so in two distinct ways.

**Faithful execution is the failure mode.** The bad post is a flawlessly executed
Story Circle: comfort zone, need, threshold, messy middle, breakthrough, cost,
return, transformation — all eight beats, in order, evenly weighted. No human
drafting from memory has ever produced that. The template's regularity *is* the
detectable artifact. Every device in the post also appears in Nick's real writing;
only the metronome was new.

**Choosing the framework chooses the meaning.** Picking Story Circle decided that
four months of tool-switching was a redemption arc — blind, then enlightened, then
home. That framing then demanded a staged epiphany, a cost paragraph, a
transformation ending, and a header called "Coming home." None of it came from the
evidence. The logs actually showed a gradual ten-day drift back, which the arc
compressed into a single Saturday morning because the shape required a turning
point.

Framework selection is an editorial judgment about what events meant. That belongs
to the person who lived them.

## What replaces it

One weak instruction, from `SKILL.md`:

> Identify the single turn this post exists to make. Arrange the facts so it lands.
> Do not impose a full arc; leave flat ground.

Most posts are already a chronology or an argument. Follow the shape in the
material.

## Legitimate uses of these files

**Diagnosing a finished draft.** "This has accidentally formed a PAS — the
agitation section is doing too much work, flatten it." "There's no turn here at
all; it's a list wearing a narrative coat."

**Naming a problem for the author.** "Your ending resolves a tension the middle
never set up" is easier to say with Freytag's vocabulary than without it.

**Answering a direct request.** If Nick says "structure this as a post-mortem,"
read `post-mortem.md` and do that. He chose the meaning; the tool is just
executing.

## What is never legitimate

Reading a framework file *before* the material is understood, then fitting facts
into its beats. That is how a lived experience turns into a template with real
timestamps in it.
